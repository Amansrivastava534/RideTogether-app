RideTogether — Privacy & location

RideTogether lets friends riding together see each other on a map.

What we collect

  • Profile: your name, optional bike name, and Google profile photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Live location (coordinates, accuracy, speed, direction, time) — only while you have turned on sharing in a ride, and only visible to approved members of that ride.
  • Ride history (optional, off by default): a simplified copy of your own route, visible only to you.
  • Push token to deliver SOS and join-request notifications.

Background location

When you share and leave “Keep sharing in the background” on, your location continues to update while the screen is off or you use other apps. Android shows a persistent notification and iOS a blue indicator while this happens. Sharing stops when you pause, stop, leave the ride, or the ride ends.

Retention

  • Live location: deleted when you stop sharing or leave; otherwise automatically within ~24 hours of the last update.
  • Ride metadata and memberships: automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • SOS alerts: automatically deleted after 7 days.
  • Ride history: automatically deleted after 90 days; you can delete it any time.

Deleting your data

Profile & settings → Delete account removes your profile, history and memberships and your sign-in account.

We do not sell data or use it for advertising. Location is never logged by our servers.
